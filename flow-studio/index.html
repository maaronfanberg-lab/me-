Mathematical model and limitations

A continuously emitted line of material points is advanced by an explicit three-dimensional vector field, using a midpoint integrator. The rendered mesh joins adjacent material points of the same emission row and adjacent emission times. The flow contains directed transport, localized vortices, shear, strain, oscillatory currents, radial deformation and optional velocity memory. It is an artistic kinematic model, not a Navier–Stokes combustion simulation or a proven strange attractor. Finite lifetime limits the displayed surface. Changing a parameter while frozen recomputes the surface from its source; no image morphing is used. Damping and feedback are chosen closures. The renderer uses real projected geometry and no blur or bloom.
