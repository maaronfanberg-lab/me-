Methods & limitations

Each accepted frame is an actual numerical trajectory in the existing fixed PCA projection. A shape screen rejects collapsed, sparse or diffuse candidates; it is a heuristic, not a guarantee of cloud-free geometry. Evolution proposes small parameter changes and warm-starts from the previous accepted state. Rejected trials are discarded. Accepted snapshots replace each other directly: no crossfade, bloom or additive accumulation. Lines join nearby consecutive samples only; they do not establish a continuous solution. Camera automatically fits the accepted geometry.

The five models are exploratory closures of supplied equations. The field is a 10-site graph proxy, with 32-step fractional memory, approximate delay, a truncated binomial fractional graph operator and conformal curvature proxy. The Bayesian layer uses a Gaussian recursive regression approximation. The ensemble uses three internal trajectories. No tanh. No image generation. Slider screening is preliminary, not a proof of chaos.

A slider can be rejected by shape screening. The last accepted value and image are restored. A high sensitivity score means projected distributions differed from the default more than a threshold in a short sweep; it does not guarantee a beautiful shape or a response at every slider position. Alternate initial states can produce different geometries.
