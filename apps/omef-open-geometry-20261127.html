Research scope and limitations

The 61 classic controls retain the original update. Optional modules implement recursive conditional coherence (100 levels plus tail), three interacting recurrences, evolving links, complex log feedback and long memory. Full 62 typed blocks, canonical string–bulk–qubit forces, tensor networks, particle filter, and original Step-14D calibration are not implemented here. The fixed-scale view uses a reference extent of six mathematical units across the shorter canvas dimension. Density accumulation counts all finite in-frame samples, not necessarily distinct pixels.
