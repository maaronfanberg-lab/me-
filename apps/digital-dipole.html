How it works

The virtual diaphragm is driven by a blend of the stereo sum and difference. Complementary filters split its signal into three upper bands. The left and right headphone channels receive independently scaled opposite-polarity components, while both receive the same protected bass from the sum. This is a simplified digital dipole model, not a measured head-related transfer function or a full critical-band model. Ordinary mono is a separate bypass. Music stays on your device.
