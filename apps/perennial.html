How to read it
  • Shape and position: the bud's true boundary, computed from f(z) = z² + c.
  • Amber: bigger than expected. Blue: smaller than expected. Brighter means a bigger surprise.
  • White outline: grown on the day shown.
  • Rings: true level curves of the bud's multiplier. More rings mean a longer period.
  • Spokes: where this bud's own children attach.
  • Stem: runs from the bud's attachment point on its parent to its centre.
  • The law: predicted radius = |dc/dλ at the root| ÷ q². Expected surprise is the average already seen in that kind of bud, so a repeating pattern stops counting as news.
