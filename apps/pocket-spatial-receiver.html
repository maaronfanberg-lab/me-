This is an experimental matrix-compatible encoder, not a licensed Dolby encoder. It preserves correlated front information while deriving a band-limited side signal, phase-rotating/delaying it, then adding it to left and subtracting it from right. That gives matrix decoders a much clearer surround-direction cue than ordinary widened stereo.
