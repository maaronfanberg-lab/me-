A concrete two-dimensional realization of the supplied equation, not a uniquely implied physical model. Here m = λ = 1, q = 2.8, and the inverse metric and Christoffel connection are calculated from g. The dynamics are integrated with RK4. Ki = 0.013 Kp; Kd = 0.18 Kp.

The L* control changes the pen's timing, not its geometric path. L* stays below 1.2 so the transformation remains nonsingular.
