Model and sound

The plasma oscillator drives the wall in one direction. Its state evolves independently of collisions. The chamber is a horizontal plane with equal-radius disks, an adjustable mass ratio, linear drag, and frictionless normal impacts. Within each simulation step the wall follows a straight segment and the next collision time is solved before advancing. The billiard study informed that event-based approach.

Each impulse excites a bank of damped modes. Hit position changes their amplitudes, the opposing walls have different pitches, and momentum transferred at impact sets the excitation. Playback begins after you enable sound.
