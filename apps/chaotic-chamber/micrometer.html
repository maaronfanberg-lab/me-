The core is a 2D numerical experiment, not actual LHC collision data or a Geant4 substitute. Charged tracks use the Lorentz law F = q(E + v × B) with a Boris velocity update, optional softened Coulomb forces, harmonic confinement, drag, and collision impulses. Particle charge signs and electron/proton/muon rest-mass ratios are preserved, but electromagnetic strengths are scaled laboratory units, not SI tesla or volts/meter. Hard disks and Gaussian sensor scattering are explicit models, not quantum scattering, annihilation or particle creation. The 240 Hz event-based collision solver approximates the bent path by short straight segments; strong fields increase this numerical error. Separate physical collisions and sensor crossings make distinct sounds. CERN tracking overview · Geant4 field integration.
