Model fidelity and numerical scope

TIDE's actual coupled equations power the field, trajectories, graph, clocks, events, landscape and audio. This is a real-time reduced discretization, not a claim of reproducing the full research-scale specification. Domain 36×36, 30 graph nodes, 32 inertial clocks, 48 Hopfield units, 56 reservoir units, with a variable-order Caputo L1 history window of 320 steps.

Explicit approximations: compressible momentum uses semi-Lagrangian advection; event arrivals use conditional Poisson time steps rather than exact Ogata thinning; Ollivier curvature uses entropically regularized transport; neural-field kernels are truncated Gaussian convolutions; RLS and fractional memory use finite numerical histories. The Information view calculates exploratory lag-one Gaussian transfer entropy from twelve sampled layer observables; it is not the full Part 15 estimator suite. Exact 256² fluid dynamics, full-spectrum Lyapunov statistics, and formal information-validation benchmarks are not claimed here.

Corrections implemented: the accumulator receives a nonlinear stabilizing loss when the documented cost alone fails; avalanche fatigue raises thresholds after a topple; Brownian strain uses squared deterministic drift, not undefined squared white-noise velocity; componentwise TIDE-vs-OMEF dynamism is not asserted without measurements. No external audio samples, arbitrary particle animations or precomputed attractor loops. The optional luminous renderer uses bilinear reconstruction, gradients and isolines of simulated fields for visual clarity and glow; it does not add numerical resolution.
