Both systems use double-precision RK4. Preset comparison states start after 120 forcing periods, so you can see the steady patterns immediately. The match test counts 16 input and 12 output peaks at drive 0.17 over 20 forcing periods, at steps 0.01 and 0.005. At drive 0.15, it counts 13 and 13 in the same window.
