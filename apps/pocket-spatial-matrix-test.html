POCKET SPATIAL · RECEIVER TEST

Tap one speaker at a time. Each button sends a stereo matrix cue intended to steer toward the named speaker through your receiver's surround decoder.
READY
Keep the receiver volume modest for the first run.
Before testing
  1. Connect the iPhone to the receiver by Bluetooth.
  2. Use Dolby Pro Logic II, Pro Logic, Matrix, or the closest surround-decoding mode. Avoid plain Stereo.
  3. Tap one speaker button repeatedly if needed and note which physical speaker actually responds.
The rear buttons are matrix encoded inside ordinary stereo. Exact separation depends on the receiver's decoder and the Bluetooth codec, so a cue may steer mostly rather than exclusively to one speaker.
