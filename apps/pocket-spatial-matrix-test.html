POCKET SPATIAL · RECEIVER TEST

Experimental stereo matrix steering for an iPhone → Bluetooth → home-theater receiver path.
READY
Keep the receiver volume modest for the first run.
Before pressing Play
  1. Connect the iPhone to the receiver by Bluetooth.
  2. On the receiver select Dolby Pro Logic II, Pro Logic, Matrix, or the closest surround-decoding mode available. Avoid plain Stereo for this test.
  3. Sit where you can identify which speaker is producing each tone.
You should hear five separate 1-second cues in this order: Front Left, Center, Front Right, Surround Right, Surround Left. The rear cues are matrix encoded inside ordinary stereo. Exact rear separation depends on your receiver decoder and the Bluetooth codec.
