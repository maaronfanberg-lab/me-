Privacy and controls

Small, capped summaries stay in this browser. Old measurements are recycled automatically.

App storageChecking…

Imported reports are summarized to avoid filling iPhone storage. Free app storage keeps your trusted devices and watchlist.

Nothing is transmitted to us. Connection checks contact this hosting website; the optional IP lookup contacts the provider named on the Monitor screen. Imported PCAP payloads aren't stored.

Alerts appear in the app while open. Safari cannot provide continuous background Wi-Fi packet capture or reliable always-on intrusion notifications.
