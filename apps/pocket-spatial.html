What the sliders mean: Spatial Amount controls the cross-ear localization cue. Virtual Speaker Angle controls its physical delay. Depth controls only the strength of four short, dark early reflections at fixed arrival times, adding a distance/room cue without changing the virtual-speaker angle. Every diagnostic above is generated from the same tested target object sent to the audio nodes.
Layer 1 · direction: each stereo channel reaches its near ear directly and the opposite ear a fraction of a millisecond later with high frequencies reduced to approximate head shadow.

Layer 2 · depth: each channel also gets two quiet, un-crossed early reflections. Left reflections arrive at 11 and 21 ms; right at 13 and 24 ms. They are filtered darker than the direct sound so they behave like room arrivals rather than extra copies of the song.
