Matrix surround: Pocket Spatial outputs a two-channel Lt/Rt-style matrix signal for compatible receiver decoding. The pseudo-object engine is signal-derived routing, not stem recovery. This is not a discrete 5.1 bitstream, Atmos output, or a licensed Dolby encoder.
Design: bass remains on the unchanged broadband front path; the extra center copy is limited to roughly 150–900 Hz, front-lock emphasis to roughly 900–3500 Hz, and rear ambience emphasis to roughly 3500–8000 Hz.
