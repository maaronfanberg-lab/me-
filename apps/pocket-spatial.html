What this first version actually does: it models two virtual speakers around your head. Each stereo channel reaches its near ear directly and the far ear a fraction of a millisecond later, with high frequencies reduced to approximate head shadow. That is real time-and-frequency processing, not automated left/right panning. It is deliberately lightweight enough for an iPhone 6.
