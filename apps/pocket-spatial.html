Experimental controls: Spatial Amount changes cross-ear energy. Speaker Angle and Delay Stretch can push localization timing beyond plausible human-head geometry. Cross-Ear Polarity can invert the delayed opposite-side path, creating strong cancellation and phase coloration. Room Time Scale stretches the early-reflection geometry, while Reflection Tone moves its damping from extremely dark to unnaturally bright.
Safety architecture: extreme settings automatically reduce the internal master gain according to the combined path gains, then pass through a high-ratio dynamics limiter before the iPhone output. This is intended to keep experimentation about spatial character rather than raw level. Your receiver or theater volume still matters.
Streaming: Audius playback uses API-accessible live streams. Pocket Spatial does not rip, export, or persist those tracks; Audius access and gating controls remain in force.
