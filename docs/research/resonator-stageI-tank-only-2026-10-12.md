Preview only (`apps/resonator-next.html`). Generated by `qa-resonator/patch_i1.py` and its revision
`patch_i2.py` (section 6–7), verified edits that abort on any mismatch. Page only: the engine source is byte-identical to the one before this
stage (the `WORKLET` source hashes to `d7c8be23432bbf40…` before and after).

**Pre-change baseline (the preview at git `fe0393fc`).**
- 8 shapes, 7 tabs (Body, Strike, Mic, Amp, FX, Mod, Learn), 8 tool buttons (orbit, strike, mic,
  move, link, pin, add, delete). 3,895 lines, 240,104 bytes.
- Moving the strike or the mic took two steps: pick the strike or mic tool, then tap a ball.
- The ring-length control was "Decay", showing its setting ("4.0 s"). On the Tank the sound actually
  takes 5.0 s to fall 40 dB and 7.4 s to fall 60 dB at that setting, so the number understated the
  ring.

**Research question.**
- Can the page become the Tank alone without changing a single sample of its sound?
- What should "Sustain" show, so the number on the knob is the ring a listener hears?
- How should a finger move the two circles on a phone, with no tool button, without fighting the
  view's own drag or the page's scrolling?

**Validation criteria (set before the change).**
- The same taps on the old and the new page send the engine the same thing: the recorded messages,
  replayed into one engine, give bit-identical sound.
- Removed controls are gone, the kept ones are all there, and nothing is wider than the phone.
- Sustain + lengthens the ring and the engine is told. From 1 s up, the readout is within 15% of the
  measured 40 dB fall time, and each longer setting rings clearly longer.
- The circles follow a finger (touch and mouse); tap-then-tap works and can be cancelled; a drag
  elsewhere turns the view and moves neither circle; two circles on one ball can both be slid off.
- Presets: a saved Tank comes back as saved; an old preset cannot switch a removed feature back on;
  codes for other shapes are refused with a reason.
- It still plays, with no script errors, in Chromium and in WebKit with iPhone emulation, and the
  earlier checks that still apply pass.

## 2–3. Sources
Fetching was not available for this note (page permissions went unanswered), so these are cited as
known references, **not re-read on 2026-10-12**:
- **Touch input.** W3C *Touch Events* (Recommendation, 2013): calling `preventDefault()` on
  `touchstart` or the first `touchmove` cancels the browser's default action (scrolling, zooming) and
  the emulated mouse events. Chrome 56 (2017) and Safari on iOS 11.3 made document-level touch
  listeners passive by default, so a listener that must cancel scrolling is registered with
  `{passive: false}`.
- **Pointer input.** W3C *Pointer Events* (Level 2 Recommendation, 2019). Safari supports them since
  version 13, so on an iPhone one finger produces both touch events and pointer events
  (`pointerType: 'touch'`).
- **Target size.** Apple's Human Interface Guidelines ask for at least 44 × 44 pt for a tappable
  control. WCAG 2.2 success criterion 2.5.8 (Target Size, Minimum) asks for at least 24 × 24 CSS px.
- **Tap or drag.** Android's `ViewConfiguration` touch slop is 8 dp: a press that moves less than that
  is a tap, not a drag.
- **Sustain.** On a piano, the sustain (damper) pedal lifts the dampers so struck strings ring on until
  it is released. Alex asked for an adjustable amount rather than on/off, so here Sustain is the
  length of the ring.
- **Decay measurement.** ISO 3382-1 defines reverberation time from the decay curve. Its T20 and T30
  are fitted over 20 and 30 dB and extrapolated to 60 dB. The readout here is a plainly defined 40 dB
  fall time, measured directly (section 5).

## 5. Mechanism
- **Removal without a new sound.**
  - Every removed control stays at its default and is still sent in the same order, so the engine
    receives what it received before.
  - The only message the new page no longer sends is `xmic` (the extra mics, which were all off
    anyway).
  - `restore()` loads Tank presets only and forces every removed control to its default (the `KEEP`
    set), so no saved preset can switch a removed feature back on.
- **Sustain.**
  - Sustain is the old Decay, the same engine parameter (`decay`), moved to the top of Body.
  - It shows "rings X s", where X = 1.25 × the setting. That is the measured time for the Tank to fall
    40 dB (to a hundredth of its strength), which the hint says in words.
- **The circles.**
  - **Input.** Touch events on the canvas (non-passive, `preventDefault`) drive the gesture. Pointer
    events of type `touch` are ignored, so one finger is one gesture, not two. Mouse and pen use
    pointer events.
  - **Slide.** A press within 30 px of a circle (a 60 px target, above both guidelines), moved more
    than 8 px, carries the circle to the nearest ball under the finger. Letting go plays the key
    (strength 0.75, 450 ms) so the change is heard.
  - **Tap, then tap.** A tap on a circle picks it up ("now tap where the strike should go"). The next
    tap puts it on the ball under the finger, even on the other circle (two circles can share a ball).
    Tapping the carried circle again puts it back down.
  - **Turning the view.** A drag that starts away from both circles turns the view. Two fingers keep
    their existing roll.
  - **Two circles on one ball.** They alternate: the one not moved last comes first.
- **Layout.** The turn arrows moved to a row at the bottom right; "reset view" is the only tool button
  left.

## 6–7. Competing explanations, corrections, limits
- **What "sounds the same" covers.** It is proved for the replayed taps: every kept control changed,
  then three notes. For other sequences the argument is structural: the engine is byte-identical,
  and the removed controls are pinned and sent as before.
- **Errors found while building and checking.** Each one is now a check.
  1. **A stall test that never stalled.**
     - One Size tap moves Size by 1/32 of its range, which usually leaves the Tank's mass count where
       it was.
     - The first Stage I version of the WebKit stall check therefore measured three "freezes" during
       which no new body was built.
     - The checks now tap until the mass count changes, and fail if it never does. On the same taps,
       the live page freezes the audio clock for 430–890 ms and the preview for 9–24 ms.
  2. **"Pitch" on a body with no clear fundamental.**
     - The Tank's lowest strong partial changed between two strikes of the same body (58.0 vs 54.1 Hz).
       Without Hush, it also carried earlier strikes: the auditions after each slide.
     - The octave-trap check now compares the five strongest spectral peaks, to 0.6 Hz, after Hush. It
       also proves its own measure: a +75-cent Trim moves all five peaks.
     - A first version, which matched within 15 cents against every peak, called a 75-cent retune
       "4 of 6 the same".
     - Mutation test: with Stage 1's fixed tuning reference removed from the engine, sliding the
       circles and one Stiffness tap retuned the Tank a fifth up (164.4 → 246.3 Hz), and the check
       failed.
  3. **Preset positions are stored to 4 decimals.** The Tank's reference mode comes back 0.02 cents
     off, so the round trip is judged at 0.5 cents (about a tenth of the smallest audible step).
  4. **A message wiped by another message's timer.** Every message now goes through `say()`.
  5. **The first Sustain readout showed the setting.** The measured 40 dB fall time is 1.25× it, so
     the readout now shows that.
  6. **Tap-then-tap could not reach a ball near the other circle.**
     - While the mic was picked up, a tap within reach of the strike circle picked up the strike
       instead.
     - WebKit CI hit it on both Linux and macOS: the mic stayed on #33, target #45. On Playwright's
       iPhone 13 the tank's canvas is 252 px tall, so the two 30 px reaches cover much of it.
     - Revision 2 (`patch_i2.py`): the next tap always puts the carried circle down.
     - The new check failed before the change and passes after it.
- **Known, not changed (Alex's decision).**
  - **Sliding the circles, then changing Size.** The new tank is tuned to the partial that is loudest
    where the circles sit. That is Stage 1's rule: modes are ranked with the strike and mic the body
    was built with.
    - Measured: after sliding the strike to #40 and the mic to #4, Size + tuned a mode 1.4 octaves
      lower on the tank's own ladder (raw 1036 vs 2822 Hz). Every partial of that new tank therefore
      came out 2.7 times higher than usual.
    - After sliding them to #22 and #47, Size − went the other way: raw 7257 vs 2313 Hz, 3.1 times
      lower.
    - So the tank as a whole can move by up to about 1.65 octaves, depending on where the circles were
      when Size changed. Sliding the circles back does not undo it until the next Size change.
    - This was there before (noted in Stage H). Stage I makes sliding the circles the main thing to
      do.
    - **A page-only fix:** build every new tank with its own default points as the tuning reference,
      then move the circles, as presets already do.
      - It changes the sound after any slide followed by a Size change.
      - It also changes some plain Size changes. At 6 rings the strike's carried-over position is #15,
        but that tank's own default point is #16.
      - So it waits for Alex.
  - **A short freeze when Size builds a new tank, in WebKit on macOS.**
    - On a CI Mac, the audio clock stood still for about 0.2 s: 99–203 ms on the preview, and
      175–181 ms on the preview before Stage I for the same taps. The page's main thread stayed free
      (gaps 11–21 ms), so it is the audio thread.
    - WebKit on Linux: 31–33 ms. Chromium: 9–24 ms.
    - It was there before Stage I (same engine, same messages). It was hidden because the stall check
      never actually built a new body (error 1).
    - The cause is not found yet. Next steps: a WebKit run with the engine's own timings, and Alex's
      phone.
  - **Leftover code.** The engine code for the removed features is still in the file, switched off and
    unreachable; it will be cut when the engine is reworked for the water tower. The page also still
    carries an older, unused Tank definition (the second one wins). That predates this stage.
- **Limits.**
  - Chromium tests the slide with real touch events through the DevTools protocol. Playwright has no
    touch-drag for WebKit, so WebKit tests the slide with the mouse and tap-then-tap with real touch
    taps. A real iPhone is the final check.
  - The 1.25× readout factor was measured on the default Tank. Across Size, Stiffness, Damping, mallet
    and key at Sustain 4, the ratio ranged 1.10–1.37, so "rings 5.0 s" means 4.4–5.5 s.
