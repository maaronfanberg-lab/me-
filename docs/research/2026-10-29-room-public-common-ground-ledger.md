# Source-grounded group memory, separate second half of Room replication (2026-10-29)

## Observed problem and research question
After the first 100-cycle trial, 20 consecutive garden turns and roughly 40 lantern turns were concrete, but some statements repeatedly rediscovered the same evidence or invented details. The private card remained in every agent's current prompt while the actual public transcript was only the most recent three turns. At published cycle 35274 Owen reintroduced his rainfall log, which he had already publicly reported at 35270; the model had lost its own earlier disclosure. The problem is not a forbidden word. It is loss of epistemic **status**: who has told whom what, and which observations are now shared.

## Foundational, newer, natural-behavior and mechanism evidence
- Clark & Wilkes-Gibbs (1986), Cognition, doi:10.1016/0010-0277(86)90010-7: interlocutors build and revise common reference through feedback and mutual evidence. This does not prove that a text ledger duplicates human memory.
- Brennan & Clark (1996), JEP:LMC, doi:10.1037/0278-7393.22.6.1482: partner-specific conceptual pacts and conventionalization show that information already shared with one person affects subsequent reference.
- Tang, Mao & Suhr (2024), EMNLP, https://aclanthology.org/2024.emnlp-main.1100/: coordinating distinct vantage points remains difficult in neural dialogue, but communicative feedback helps.
- Abdulhai et al., NeurIPS 2025, https://proceedings.neurips.cc/paper_files/paper/2025/hash/4c91443877f8388d8190c938ac5a4d4d-Abstract-Conference.html: multi-turn line-to-line and question-answer consistency track role-play quality better than single-turn impression.
- A verified, read-only Claude Sonnet engineering review (bridge/outbox/room-dialogue-scope-review-20261029-1210.json) independently identified missing persistent event/source status. An earlier bridge request failed and is not evidence.

## Proposed mechanism and technical mapping
For the **later half only** of the already registered rainwater replication (pre-state cycles **35280–35291**), load the actual published feed after the scene onset and extract at most one source-bearing, shortened **real public utterance** for each of four characters when it contains >=2 distinctive content terms from that speaker's own personal observation. Present only the **verbatim public utterance**, not the private card, in a clearly labeled shared-evidence channel after the latest turn and before the active scene notice. A character's source card alone does not qualify as shared. This new contextual memory may help the agents treat previously voiced facts as common background rather than repeatedly presenting them as discoveries. All speech remains generated by the local model, not a script. Visitor messages take priority; missing/garbled feed causes the ledger to fail closed; no hidden agent facts are leaked.

## Competing explanations and limitations
The matching uses superficial content-term overlap and can yield false negatives on paraphrases or false positives when two ordinary terms overlap. Returning public text from older history does not guarantee the model correctly understands epistemic commitments, and may amplify copying or repeated phrases. The context token budget is limited, but at most four published snippets of <=240 chars are added. The change can make small models repeat the *ledger* instead of discussing new facts; successful CI would not rule this out. The phase is **not randomized** and has 12 versus 12 turns in a single evolving rainwater scene, with carry-over, speaker distribution and model seed changing across turns. Old and new conditions differ as time moves on, so evidence is exploratory.
