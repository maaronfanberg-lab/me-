# Room hidden-world ablation: preregistration (2026-10-29)

Observed: Room resumed after runtime repair but many real turns continued to compare conversations with birds, woven threads and metaphors. This is not substantively responsive topic development.

Hypothesis: partial, independently held, source-labelled information creates natural questions, answers, corrections and substantive interaction. Alternative: a shared concrete task alone suffices; another alternative is that model context/quality dominates both.

Evidence: Sacks, Schegloff and Jefferson, Language 50 (1974) 696-735, doi:10.2307/412243 (turn-taking and recipient design); Clark and Wilkes-Gibbs, Cognition 22 (1986) 1-39, doi:10.1016/0010-0277(86)90010-7 (interactive collaborative grounding); Park et al., UIST 2023, doi:10.1145/3586183.3606763 (generative agents with observations and memory; limited simulation evidence).

Research gate: (1) baseline public transcript observed; (2) established conversation analysis reviewed; (3) contemporary simulated-agent study checked; (4) natural conversation evidence concerns repair and reciprocal information; (5) proposed mechanism is bounded private clues shared only via public utterance; (6) alternative task framing/model limits identified; (7) three-condition ablation with equal seeds/speaker opportunities, evaluate limitations; (8) greenhouse case is explicitly fictional and cannot prove broad spontaneous human conversational behavior; (9) map to isolated experiment only, no production or fabricated memories; (10) require actual model-generated dialogue and sustained responsive novel topic before production.

Three conditions: no clues, identical clues for everyone, and private observations. Measure speaker participation, questions, cross-agent information disclosure, actual response to another's evidence, empty meta talk, hallucinations, and silence. Initial 16 turns per condition is pilot only, not success. Extend to 100+ turns and independently review transcript before any deployment. Preserve real Allen messages and no fake past incidents. Post-change result: pending model ablation; not deployed.

## Initial model pilot: real 1B speech (GitHub Actions run 40861365363)
The first isolated 16-opportunity-per-condition model run completed successfully. Empty evidence: 15 published speech candidates, 0 questions, no objective clue disclosures. Shared full evidence: 14 utterances, 2 questions, all four fact-owner markers appeared. Hidden/private evidence: 16 utterances, 3 questions, markers from only Sarah and Owen. All four voices appeared in each condition. The hidden condition sometimes produced third-person stage directions and recycled generic inspections instead of sharing Mara's door evidence or Jules's footprint evidence. Therefore the early pilot does NOT establish that privacy alone improves real dialogue. It illustrates that giving a concrete shared external situation already changes the subject from meta-conversation, but the small model still repeats generic suggestions. The next grounded hidden condition adds distinct witnessed evidence and an explicit observable distinction between testimony and speculation. The subsequent full 100 opportunity comparison is pending; no experimental utterance is published to the live Room.
