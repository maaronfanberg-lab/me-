# RESONATOR Stage F — plate reverb (2026-10-12)

Preview only (`apps/resonator-next.html`). Generated by `qa-resonator/patch_f1.py` (the reverb) and
`qa-resonator/patch_f2.py` (calibration from measurement). Both are verified edits that abort on any
mismatch.

## 1. Observed problem
Anukari's FX object offers 27 effects, reverb among them (anukari.com, read 2026-10-11).
RESONATOR's own effects were space (image, distance, early reflections, Doppler, rotation, elevation)
and the attractor's chorus, delay, wow, tremolo and drive. It had no reverberation tail. Alex asked
for as much of Anukari as runs on the phone.

**Research question.** Can a reverb be added that does what its controls say (time, tone, pre-delay)
and is cheap enough for the phone? It must leave the sound unchanged, bit for bit, when it is off.

**Pre-change baseline.** No reverb. The early reflections ("Reflections", four taps) were the only
room cue.

**Validation criteria (set before the change).**
- Off is bit-identical.
- Measured T60 within 5% of the setting (5% is the subjective limen for reverberation time, ISO 3382-1
  Annex A).
- The tone control behaves as its filter predicts.
- Left and right are decorrelated.
- Pre-delay is exact.
- Stable at the longest setting.
- 100% is about as loud as the dry sound.
- True peak at or below −1 dBTP.
- Deterministic.
- Hush silences it below audibility.
- A new body keeps the tail.
- Every control works by tap, in Chromium and in WebKit.

## 2–3. Sources (read 2026-10-12)
- **Primary.** J. Dattorro, "Effect Design, Part 1: Reverberator and Other Filters", *J. Audio Eng. Soc.*
  45(9), 1997 (ccrma.stanford.edu/~dattorro/EffectDesignPart1.pdf). Taken from it:
  - Table 1: Fs = 29761 Hz; decay 0.50; decay diffusion 1 = 0.70; decay diffusion 2 = decay + 0.15,
    floor 0.25, ceiling 0.50; input diffusion 0.750 / 0.625; bandwidth 0.9995; damping 0.0005;
    excursion 16.
  - Table 2: the 14 output taps, each × 0.6.
  - The decay-diffusion-1 lattices take a negative coefficient.
  - The two delay lines at the tank's head are modulated.
- **Cross-check of the delay lengths** (the paper's figure did not extract legibly). The freeverb3
  technical notes, "matlab reverb" Figure 5 (freeverb3-vst.sourceforge.io/doc/matlab_reverb.pdf),
  give:
  - input allpasses 142, 107, 379, 277;
  - left tank 672 (modulated allpass), 4453, 1800 (allpass), 3720;
  - right tank 908, 4217, 2656, 3163.

  Its tap table matches Dattorro's Table 2. Third check: every tap position lies inside the line it
  reads.
- **Measurement method.** Schroeder backward integration; T60 from the −5 to −35 dB range (T30),
  ISO 3382-1.

## 5. Mechanism
- **Topology.** Dattorro's figure-of-eight tank:
  - Mono input, through pre-delay, a bandwidth low-pass and four input allpasses.
  - Two cross-coupled halves, each: a modulated allpass, a delay, a damping low-pass, decay, an allpass,
    another delay, decay.
  - Stereo out from 7 taps per side.
- **Scaling.** Lengths are scaled from 29761 Hz to the engine rate.
- **Modulation.** 1 Hz, in quadrature left and right, with linear interpolation.
- **Placement.** After everything else (space, the attractor's effects, extra mics, the per-shape level)
  and before the true-peak limiter.
- **Reverb time.** One trip round the eight is 21589 samples at 29761 Hz, with decay applied four
  times. So decay = 0.001^(trip / (4·T60)) (derived; Dattorro's decay 0.50 gives 1.81 s).
- **Measured, the formula runs long** by 4–10% above 0.6 s. At 0.3 s it gives 0.52 s, because a trip
  takes 0.73 s and the plate cannot ring meaningfully shorter.
  - The long bias has a physical reason: an allpass's group delay at low frequency is N(1+g)/(1−g).
    That is 3N for the 0.5 diffusers, so the slowest parts of the tail decay more slowly than the
    trip-time average.
  - The measured ratio fits 1 + 0.0907·T^−0.29 within 1.5% over 0.6–12 s. The engine solves for the
    design time that measures as the setting. The knob's minimum is 0.6 s.
- **Tone.** One-pole damping, d = exp(−2π·fc/fs), twice per trip.
- **Level.** The reverb alone at 100% measured 2.66 dB above the dry sound; a fixed gain of 0.736
  brings it to match.
- **Hush.** Clears the tank when Hush is pressed, and again when the body resets at the end of Hush's
  10 ms fade (the fade had fed the cleared tank again).
- **A new body (shape change).** Re-sends every knob, so the engine clears the tank only when the reverb
  is switched on from off. A ringing tail survives a shape change.

## 6–7. Competing explanations, corrections, limits
- **My first tone check failed against an arbitrary threshold.** Compared over a narrow band at
  4.5 kHz with the filter's own prediction, it agrees within 7%. The wide-band (3–8 kHz) measurement
  was dominated by its slowest edge.
- **My first Hush check failed at a 1e-6 bar.** The residue is −99 to −108 dBFS (the output filters'
  last millionths echoing into the cleared tank). Without the reverb it is ~1e-11. The bar is now
  audibility, −90 dBFS.
- **Ratchet:** the P1 proof swept the *new* page's knobs and flagged Reverb as a difference. A
  bit-identity sweep must use the baseline's controls (fixed and documented).
- **Limits.**
  - Linear interpolation of the modulated lines slightly dulls the highest frequencies; Dattorro
    discusses allpass interpolation as the alternative.
  - Reverb is not a modulation destination.
  - It is one plate, not a choice of rooms.

## 8. Context transfer
The algorithm is deterministic arithmetic, identical in V8 and JavaScriptCore (the Stage P WebKit runs
showed the engine bit-identical across them). Cost on this machine: +1.3% of real time for String
(5.9 → 7.2%). It is not measured on a phone; the phone's own CPU reading is in Copy report.

## 9. Implementation mapping
- **Engine.**
  - New fields: `rvMix`, `rvTime`, `rvTone`, `rvPre`, `rvK`.
  - New methods: `rvSet()`, `rvAlloc()`, `rvClear()`, `reverbSample()` and line helpers. The buffers
    are made on first use.
  - Hooks:
    - the per-sample output, after the shape level and before the limiter;
    - `hush` and `hardReset()` clear the tank;
    - knob messages call `rvSet()`.
- **Page.**
  - FX tab → "Plate reverb": Reverb, Reverb time, Reverb tone, Pre-delay, each with −/+ and hints.
  - The three explicit knob lists (new body, Scramble, rebuild) include them.
  - Presets save them. A preset from before reverb loads dry.
