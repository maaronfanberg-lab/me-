Research record 2026-10-29. Hypothesis: action-dependent observable consequences can advance talk beyond repeated proposals. Alternatives: shared task alone or model limitations explain observations. Foundational sources: Sacks et al. 1974 turn-taking; Clark and Wilkes-Gibbs 1986 collaborative grounding; Park et al. 2023 generative agents. Tests: same speaker schedule and model sampling, 20-turn passive-versus-causal pilot, explicit first-person commitments only, no fabricated public speech, world facts remain isolated until observed. Limits: fictional small world, 1B model, not proof of human behavior or live deployment. Before production: assess real transcripts for new verified information uptake and sustained development over 100+ turns; separate live feed verification required.
