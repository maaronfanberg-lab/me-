# RESONATOR Stage 5 — envelopes and envelope followers (2026-10-11)

Preview only (`apps/resonator-next.html`); generated by `qa-resonator/patch_s5.py`.

## 1. Observed problem
Anukari's modulators include "MIDI-triggered envelopes" and "envelope followers that turn mic amplitude
into a control signal" (anukari.com/features, read 2026-10-11). The release notes add more:
- Trigger Envelopes (0.9.6, 2025-05-27).
- "Fixed waveform discontinuities when envelopes were re-triggered, which could cause pops/clicks"
  (0.9.23, 2025-12-13).
- "Envelope Followers now have a configurable delay" (0.9.31, 2026-05-13).

RESONATOR had three LFOs only. Found while reading the modulator code: turning a modulator's Depth to 0
left its destination stuck at the last modulated value. Measured: Pitch stays +455 cents after an LFO
at 50% is turned to 0. The live page has the same fault.

**Research question.** Can each modulator also be an envelope or a follower, with times a player can
trust, while LFOs stay exactly as they are?

## 2–5. Evidence and mechanism
- **Envelope semantics.** These follow Puckette, *Theory and Technique of Electronic Music*,
  "Envelope generators" (ch. 4):
  - attack and decay are durations, sustain is a level, and "off" starts the release;
  - an early "off" cancels whatever remains of the attack and decay;
  - it can be retriggered before the release ends, even during the attack.

  Here a retrigger continues from the current value. Anukari 0.9.23 fixed clicks caused by retrigger
  discontinuities; measured, the largest per-sample step is 0.0026.
- **Envelope curves.** These are analog-style exponential segments that aim past their goal, so each
  one arrives in its set time: attack aims at 1.5, decay at Sustain − 0.05 and release at −0.05.
  This is an engineering choice, not taken from a primary source. Its timing is derived and proven:
  attack 9.97 ms (set 10), decay 100.03 ms (set 100), release from 50% 157.50 ms (derived 157.52).
- **Gate.** Every key starts the envelopes, and they let go when the last key comes up. Held keys are
  tracked by pitch. Envelopes are global: every voice shares one, and every key retriggers it.
- **Follower detector.** This is the smooth decoupled peak detector from Giannoulis, Massberg & Reiss,
  "Digital Dynamic Range Compressor Design — A Tutorial and Analysis" (JAES 60(6), 399–408, 2012). The
  paper recommends it for artifact-free use: its release has no discontinuities and it colours timbre
  least. It uses one-pole coefficients α = e^(−1/(τ·fs)) (their Eq. 7). Its step response matches
  the closed form: 63% rise 10.00 ms for τA = 10 ms; fall to 37% 70.88 ms, against 70.91 ms from
  y = (τR e^(−t/τR) − τA e^(−t/τA))/(τR − τA).
- **Follower defaults (22 ms attack, 50 ms release).** These come from Glasberg & Moore, "A model of
  loudness applicable to time-varying sounds" (JAES 50(5), 331–342, 2002). Its short-term loudness
  averages with attack and release coefficients of 0.045 and 0.02 per 1-ms step (as reported by Moore,
  quoted in arXiv:1801.09900), which is about 22 ms and 50 ms. So a follower at the defaults tracks
  loudness changes roughly as ears do.
- **What a follower hears.** It hears a mic as the listener hears it, after the per-shape level trim.
  Without the trim, the Tank read 5× quieter than the Chime even though the shapes are matched in
  loudness. Sources are Mics 1–4 (Stage 4) and the phone microphone (Stage 3's Mic in). The phone
  microphone is read even with Mic input at 0, so a voice can steer without striking the body.
- **Default sensitivity (+13 dB).** For a firm hit (velocity 0.9), measured detector peaks are
  0.16–0.24 on seven shapes and 0.50 on the Tank. A gain of 4.47 takes a firm hit to 0.70–1.00 on
  every shape, and a softer hit (0.55) proportionally less.
- **Natural behaviour this enables.** Real drums and strings rise in pitch with amplitude (tension
  modulation), and the glide falls as the sound decays (Marogna, Avanzini & Bank, DAFx-10, 2010). A
  follower on Stiffness produces that kind of behaviour as a control signal. It is not a physical
  tension model; the Nonlinearity knob is the physical route.
- **Direction.** Envelope and follower outputs run from 0 to 1, and Push up / Push down sets which
  way they move the destination.
- **Meters.** The engine's existing ~34 Hz frame carries the three values, but only when an envelope
  or follower exists.
- **Depth 0 fix.** Depth 0 now returns the destination to its own setting.

## 6–8. Competing explanations, limits, context
- **Intended difference from live.** The Depth-0 fix is the only intended change from the live page.
  LFO messages are unchanged, and 20 LFO setups render bit-identical to live.
- **Global envelopes.** Envelopes are not per voice; Anukari's instanced polyphony gives each voice its
  own. Pitch modulation is applied once per audio block (about 2.7 ms). That was already true for
  LFOs; Anukari fixed similar stepping in 1.0.3.
- **Measured behaviours.**
  - A 5 ms attack on Level lets the first milliseconds of a hit through, as the attack time implies.
  - The meter shows the current value about every 32 ms, so the peak of a fast pluck reads about
    0.8–0.9.
- **Not built yet.** Follower delay (Anukari 0.9.31) — possible later.
- **Feedback loops stay bounded.** A follower on Mic 1 can drive Level or Feedback. The control is
  clamped to 0–1, destinations are clamped, and the limiter holds: worst case 0.767 against the 0.78
  limit.
- **Errors in my own tests, fixed.**
  - The meter check expected 0.9 at the peak. Frame sampling makes 0.80 the correct floor.
  - The audible check measured a peak that included the mallet onset, before the 5 ms attack. It now
    measures the held note.
- **Phone.** Per-sample cost is a few multiplies per modulator. Not measured on a phone.

## 9. Implementation mapping
- **Engine.**
  - New modulator fields: type, value, stage and coefficients.
  - The `mod` message gains `ty`, `inv`, `src`, `A`, `D`, `S`, `R`, `fa`, `fr` and `sens`.
  - `modStep()` advances envelopes and followers. `envNote()` runs from note and note-off, and hush
    resets everything.
  - Follower inputs: Mic 1 after the trim, Mics 2–4, and the phone mic.
  - The meter values ride in the existing frame message. Depth 0 restores the destination.
- **Page (Mod tab, per modulator).**
  - LFO / Envelope / Follower chips.
  - Envelope: Attack, Decay and Release (1 ms – 10 s), and Sustain (0–100%).
  - Follower: Mic 1–4 / Phone mic chips, Follow attack, Follow release, and Sensitivity (0 to +36 dB).
  - Push up / Push down, a meter, and hints.
  - Presets carry type, direction, source and the knobs.
