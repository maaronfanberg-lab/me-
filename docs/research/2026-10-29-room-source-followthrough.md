# Room follow-through: unfulfilled intentions are not completed actions (October 29, 2026)

## Observation and falsifiable hypothesis
The first live choir scene (cycles 36290–36314) involved concrete observations, but several characters repeatedly said “I'll check”, “I'm going to move”, “I'll try pushing” without publicly recording the corresponding action. Others continued proposing the same steps. A separate bug blocked “I just moved” from producing an environmental effect; that parser has now been corrected in PR #370. The remaining issue is the lack of persistent, source-linked state about intentions which have been publicly expressed but not completed. This is a hypothesis about continuity, not a guarantee of human dialogue.

## Research gate: natural conversation / competing evidence
Sacks, Schegloff & Jefferson (1974), Language 50, DOI 10.2307/412243: turns are locally organized around participant response relevance, rather than a fixed topical schedule. Clark & Wilkes-Gibbs (1986), Cognition 22, DOI 10.1016/0010-0277(86)90010-7: grounding and collaborative action depend on mutually established reference and ongoing adjustments. Brennan & Clark (1996), JEP:LMC 22, DOI 10.1037/0278-7393.22.6.1482: partner-specific conceptual pacts reflect what has been established in interaction. These studies do *not* validate a promise ledger for an autonomous, local 3B LLM. “I'll check” may be a polite offer rather than a binding commitment, the parser may miss paraphrases or falsely count an intention, and increasing reminders could make conversation robotic or nagging. Small-agent simulation limitations noted by Zhou et al. (EMNLP 2024, https://aclanthology.org/2024.emnlp-main.1208/) counsel restraint.

## Mechanism
Introduce a *bounded, source-identified unfulfilled-intention memory*. A public, first-person, affirmative planned action referring to an actual scene object is kept separately from a witnessed action. Only that same actor's later published completed action closes their own plan. The generator receives at most three actual public excerpts, each labeled as a spoken intention **not proof of action**, and remains free to act, refuse, disagree, change topic, or do nothing. The world-effect parser remains independently authoritative. No synthetic character words, no mandatory tasks, no team turn rotation, no banned topic vocabulary.

## Controlled implementation
`scripts/room_followthrough.py` derives action and object grammar from the existing scene-effect definitions, refuses questions, hypothetical suggestions, and visitor input, and strictly checks speaker/source IDs, per-scene onset, and time. The model prompt includes its notice only if flag `ROOM_PLAN_FOLLOWTHROUGH=1` and pre-state cycle is in [36328,36364) (the greenhouse scene), distinct from the earlier choir baseline [36280,36328). This compares environments as an exploratory within-run experiment, **not** a randomized trial. Existing scene effect/status code and visitor priority remain unchanged. The separate planetarium trial [36480,36600) is unaffected.

## Alternative explanations and bounded risks
A 3B model may favor proposals due to weights or lack of situational understanding; persistence may not make it execute anything. Input exact-object matching may miss colloquial “cord” or synonyms. Scene changes, source-history carryover and run restart can produce changes unrelated to this implementation. If a source-history turn has no ID or is outside the scene, it is ignored rather than inventing memory. Published plans are reports, not guarantees; treat them as flexible references. If prompt-structured reminders become repetitive, revert the flag.

## Validation and 10-level checklist
1 Observe real published non-completed plans; 2–4 rely on established human ground/sequence organization with explicit limited transfer; 5 mechanism is cross-turn source-linked intent tracking; 6–7 alternative small-model inertia and parsing biases; 8 explicitly non-generalizable 4-model fictional scene; 9 smallest added module, prompt gate, CI regression; 10 compare source-tagged pending plans, actual completed actions, question-to-answer relationships, and factual scene progress.
CI requires: hypothetical and future suggestions cannot become physical effects, same actor completion clears only their own earlier plan, no future source inclusion, no visitor leakage, no stale-scene leakage, prompt integration, and finite expiration. A successful run must show some new actual follow-through and shared reaction, not merely more plans. Do not claim success from merge alone.
