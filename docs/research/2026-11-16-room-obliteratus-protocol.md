# Research record: Room and OBLITERATUS v0.1.4, 2026-11-16

**Gate: research-only harness permitted. Production promotion blocked.**

Research question: Can validated non-refusal concept steering alter conversational initiative in a controlled, independently evaluated model without reversible-hook leakage? A change need not be an improvement. All outputs are synthetic, and a null or negative finding is valid.

Ten-level repository research gate:

1. **Observed problem:** The user reports Room stalls, repetition and fragile continuity. Room's historical pilot research documents excess talking, reflection loops, and insufficient sustained human-like behavior. None demonstrate refusal as the cause.
2. **Foundations:** Arditi et al. (2024), https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.11717, and Turner et al. (2023), https://arxiv.org/abs/2308.10248 support the possibility of targeted activation interventions, not better real-world conversation.
3. **Current evidence:** OBLITERATUS v0.1.4, 2026-10-15, commit 461ff086e675d93969bbd7c99c53505762452309; tag-locked documentation on concept packs, validation and interventions.
4. **Natural behavior:** Turn taking, conversational repair, partner-specific grounding, responsiveness, and mutual history require interactional mechanisms over time. Six invented fixtures cannot measure them fully.
5. **Mechanism:** Fit a validated contrastive direction for a specific non-refusal conversational feature with semantic controls, apply it reversibly at inference, and compare identical deterministic inputs.
6. **Competing explanations:** Prompt wording, lexical style confounds, verbosity, decoding, format and ordinary model variation may shift the visible answer without genuine responsiveness.
7. **Replication/limitations:** Effects can be non-monotonic; OBLITERATUS says representational separability does not establish behavior changes. Independent data, multiple seeds, models, safety tests and null reporting are needed.
8. **Context transfer:** Dense Transformers and a short single-speaker dialogue cannot establish applicability to production quantized GGUF or long-term five-person Room interaction.
9. **Mapping:** experiments/room-obliteratus/ab_trial.py is isolated. No production script, public feed, memory, prompts, workflow schedule or service is imported or modified.
10. **Post-change validation:** Six zero-dependency harness tests assess controls, alpha switching, repeat detection, fixture validation and restoration-failure reporting. Real-model inference, full refusal-collateral evaluation, KV-cache tests and blind naturalness assessments remain unperformed.

**Pre-change experimental baseline:** Not measured; previous Room canaries do not offer a matched baseline.

**Result so far:** An isolated experimental harness and independent, fictional conversation cases. No true OBLITERATUS steering trial or live-room effect observed. The user's ZIP could not be downloaded for local hash verification; upstream source APIs were inspected at exact tag v0.1.4.

**Go/no-go:** Require a validated direction and identical model provenance, alpha=0 and post-hook restoration, multiple held-out replications, neutral-or-better coherence and safety collateral, and blinded longer-term conversation assessment. Currently blocked.
