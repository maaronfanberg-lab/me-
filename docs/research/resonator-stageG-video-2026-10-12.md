# RESONATOR Stage G — video with sound (2026-10-12)

Preview only (`apps/resonator-next.html`). Generated by `qa-resonator/patch_g1.py` (recording) and
`qa-resonator/patch_g2.py` (opaque background while recording). Both are verified edits.

## 1. Observed problem
Anukari records "MP4 video of the 3D viewport with audio, optionally including the DAW mix"
(anukari.com/features, read 2026-10-11). RESONATOR could record sound only (a WAV of the output).
Alex asked for as much of Anukari as runs on the phone.

**Research question.** Can the phone record the 3D view and the sound together, as a file it can keep
in Photos, without touching the sound or the engine?

**Pre-change baseline.**
- Record / Save made a WAV only.
- Outside the orbit tool the 3D view redraws at most 10 times a second, to save power.

**Validation criteria (set before the change).**
- The file has exactly one video and one audio stream, the right length, the sound in it, and frames
  showing the body.
- The browser can decode its own recording.
- Saving hands over a named file.
- A time limit stops a recording.
- The instrument still plays afterwards.
- A browser without MediaRecorder explains instead of failing.
- No engine change at all, so engine proofs are untouched.

## 2–3. Sources (read 2026-10-12)
- **WebKit blog**, "MediaRecorder API" (Y. Fablet, 2020-11-23): enabled by default from iOS 14.3.
  "Safari currently supports the MP4 file format with H.264 as video codec and AAC as audio codec."
  The recorder takes any MediaStreamTrack, including tracks "generated from HTML (Canvas, WebAudio)".
  Formats can be feature-detected.
- **WebKit bug 181663**, "Video Element cannot playback local Canvas.captureStream on iOS". Resolved
  (configuration changed), 2022-05-16. Comments mention remaining issues with transparent pixels and
  frame rate; the duplicate bug 229611 covered blank MediaRecorder output from a 2D canvas.
- **Apple Developer Forums 729782.** On iOS 16 the share sheet lacks "Save to Photos" for shared
  *images*, but sharing a *video* works. Thread 694207 reports MediaRecorder's `onstop` not firing on
  iOS 15.
- **web.dev**, "Integrate with the OS sharing UI with the Web Share API": `navigator.share({files})`
  and `canShare`.

## 5. Mechanism
- **Video.** `canvas.captureStream(30)` of the 3D view. While recording, the view redraws every frame
  (the 10-a-second power saving is lifted), and the stage's gradient is painted into the canvas.
  On screen it is a CSS background behind a transparent canvas, so a capture would be black there.
  The opaque background also sidesteps WebKit's transparent-pixel issue.
- **Sound.** A `MediaStreamAudioDestinationNode` on the same master gain that feeds the speakers.
  The video hears exactly what you hear, after the engine's limiter.
- **Recorder.** MP4 with H.264/AAC where offered (iPhone), otherwise the best the browser offers
  (Chrome: MP4 with VP9/Opus, or WebM). 2.5 Mbit/s video, 192 kbit/s audio, 1 s chunks.
- **Stopping.** Requests the last data, and finalizes after 2 s even if `onstop` never fires (iOS 15).
- **Caption.** Drawn on the view while recording: a red dot and the time (top right), and
  "RESONATOR · shape · masses" (bottom left). It shows on screen that recording is on, and labels the
  video.
- **Saving.** The share sheet with the file (iPhone: "Save Video" puts it in Photos), otherwise a
  download link. Up to 60 s per recording.

## 6–7. Competing explanations, corrections, limits
- **Test artefact.** The first "frame is not black" check shrank the frame to 64 px wide, which
  averaged the small balls and caption away (0.06% bright). At full resolution the same frame has 0.9%
  bright pixels, a non-black mean and real structure (spread 18). The check now uses full resolution
  (recorded in `qa-resonator/README.md`).
- **Limits.**
  - Only the 3D view is in the video (the readout and buttons are page elements, not canvas).
  - It records the instrument's output only, not the phone mic.
  - A black or blank video on some iOS versions cannot be ruled out from here (WebKit's history above).
    Only the phone can confirm.

## 8. Context transfer
Chromium writes VP9/Opus; the iPhone will write H.264/AAC, the format Photos expects. The WebKit CI
run checks Safari's engine on Linux and macOS where its build offers MediaRecorder; missing
MediaRecorder is reported as a note, not a pass.

## 9. Implementation mapping
- **Page only.**
  - `VREC` state; `vrecStart()`, `vrecStop()`, `vrecFinish()`, `vrecSave()`, `vrecOverlay()`,
    `vrecBg()`; Record video and Save video on the Strike tab, with a note line.
  - `frame()` skips its 10-a-second throttle and draws the background and overlay while recording.
- **Engine:** unchanged.
