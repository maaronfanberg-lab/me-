# RESONATOR Stage 2 — free building (2026-10-11)

Preview only (`apps/resonator-next.html`); generated by `qa-resonator/patch_s2.py`.

## 1. Observed problem
Anukari's core is free construction: drag in masses, anchors and springs ("hundreds of objects",
anukari.com/features). RESONATOR had link (springs) and pin (anchors) but no way to add or delete a
mass, so every instrument was one of 8 templates. Changing Size or shape also cleared undo, so any
hand edit could be lost with one tap.

## 5. Mechanism / implementation mapping
- **add**: tap empty space. The screen point is inverted through the camera (rotation matrix, so
  inverse = transpose; same perspective factor as `project()`), at the depth of the nearest mass on
  screen. The new mass is wired to its nearest neighbour in 3D: with no spring it has no restoring
  force from the structure and cannot be excited by it.
- **delete**: tap a mass; its springs go and indices are renumbered. A deletion can cut a body in
  two. A strike or mic that loses its mass moves to the nearest mass on its partner's piece, so the
  pair stays connected; the second mic goes as far from the first as the struck piece allows. If an
  unrelated deletion separates strike and mic, they stay put and the page says so.
- **limit** 100 masses: background tuning is O(n³) in degrees of freedom; 100 masses ≈ 300 dof.
  Not a phone measurement. The Copy report shows the slowest tuning time on the actual phone.
- **undo** now covers Size and shape changes; each body carries its own shape and size, so a
  snapshot can't pair one body with another body's label.
- **engine guard**: the second mic is re-placed if its mass no longer exists or is pinned.

## 6–7. Competing explanations and limits found while testing
- A silent right channel after deletions looked like a stuck filter (the second mic's reset routine
  clears `lp2B` but not `lp2`). Offline replay showed valid (non-NaN) filter values and the real
  cause: the deletion cut the string and the second mic sat on the piece the mallet never touches.
  Fixed by the connectivity-aware placement above; the `lp2` reset asymmetry was left alone (no
  evidence of harm, and changing it would alter existing behaviour).
- **Known modelling limit (for Stage 4, mics):** the pickup coil aperture spans neighbouring
  *mass numbers*, not neighbouring masses in space. On the templates these coincide; on hand-built
  bodies the coil can "hear" across a cut. Planned fix: aperture over spring-connected neighbours.
- My first undo implementation recorded the new shape label with the old body (the Size knob and
  shape buttons update the label before rebuilding); caught by the browser check and fixed.
