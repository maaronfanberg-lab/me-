Drag to rotate · pinch or scroll to magnify · two fingers to pan. Evolve changes mathematical field parameters smoothly; Freeze holds the exact shape. Lock any geometry control to exclude it from evolution.

Numerical 3D samples of thin material layers transformed by localized differential rotations and shears. Optical depth is accumulated from the projected particles. No image textures, AI imagery or smoke simulation. CPU mode uses fewer samples during evolution and slider movement, then restores the selected count when stopped. This prototype uses prescribed flow maps, not a full self-interacting vortex solver.
