Model scope. This experimental sculptural instrument solves a 3D complex Ginzburg–Landau field with field-induced advection, an adaptive phase-oscillator network, a memory field, and a closed feedback string. Its volumetric surfaces are numerically extracted level sets of the evolving 3D field; filaments are integrated 3D tracer paths. The algorithms are an OMEF-inspired reduced coupling, not the full 92-page master model. Audio requires a tap to start on iPhone. Mathematical controls change solver coefficients; contour and material controls change only the expression of that state.
